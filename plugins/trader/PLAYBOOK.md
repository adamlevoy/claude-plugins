# Pattern Playbook

**Detailed definitions of tradeable patterns with entry/exit rules.**

---

## Inverse Head & Shoulders (iH&S)

### Description
Reversal pattern forming after downtrend. Three troughs with middle (head) lower than the two shoulders. Signals trend reversal when neckline breaks.

### Identification Criteria
- [ ] Clear prior downtrend
- [ ] Left shoulder forms, rally to resistance
- [ ] Head forms lower than left shoulder
- [ ] Right shoulder forms higher than head (ideally similar height to left)
- [ ] Neckline connects the two peaks between shoulders
- [ ] Volume: Higher on left shoulder, lowest on head, increasing into breakout

### Entry Rules
- **Trigger**: Close above neckline on increased volume
- **Aggressive**: Enter on neckline test after initial break
- **Conservative**: Wait for retest of neckline as support

### Stop Placement
- Below right shoulder low
- Or below neckline (tighter, higher risk of stop-out)

### Target Calculation
- **Measured Move**: Distance from head to neckline, projected up from breakout
- **T1**: 50% of measured move
- **T2**: 100% of measured move

### Ideal Setup
- Consolidation: 6+ weeks
- Volume confirmation on breakout (1.5x+ average)
- Neckline relatively horizontal (not too steep)
- Right shoulder higher than left (shows buyers getting aggressive)

## Cup & Handle

### Description
Continuation pattern after uptrend. Rounded "cup" bottom followed by small "handle" consolidation before breakout to new highs.

### Identification Criteria
- [ ] Prior uptrend (at least 30% move)
- [ ] Cup forms with rounded bottom (not V-shaped)
- [ ] Cup depth: 12-35% from peak to trough
- [ ] Handle forms in upper third of cup
- [ ] Handle drifts down slightly (not more than 50% of cup depth)
- [ ] Handle duration: 1-4 weeks typically

### Entry Rules
- **Trigger**: Break above handle resistance (cup's prior high)
- **Volume**: Must exceed average on breakout

### Stop Placement
- Below handle low
- Or below middle of handle (tighter)

### Target Calculation
- **Measured Move**: Cup depth projected up from breakout
- **T1**: Prior resistance levels
- **T2**: Full measured move

### Ideal Setup
- Cup duration: 7-65 weeks
- Handle forms after right side of cup completes
- Volume dry-up in handle
- Breaking out in direction of larger trend

## Falling Wedge

### Description
Bullish pattern with converging trendlines both sloping down. Can be reversal or continuation. Breakout is upward.

### Identification Criteria
- [ ] Two converging trendlines, both sloping down
- [ ] At least 3 touches on each trendline
- [ ] Price making lower highs AND lower lows (but converging)
- [ ] Volume decreasing as pattern develops
- [ ] Pattern typically takes 3-6 weeks minimum

### Entry Rules
- **Trigger**: Close above upper trendline
- **Confirmation**: Volume spike on breakout
- **Aggressive**: Enter on first candle close above line
- **Conservative**: Wait for retest of broken trendline

### Stop Placement
- Below most recent swing low in wedge
- Or below lower trendline

### Target Calculation
- **Measured Move**: Height of wedge at widest point, projected from breakout
- **T1**: First major resistance above
- **T2**: Full measured move

### Ideal Setup
- Clear, clean trendlines (not sloppy)
- Tight apex (lines converging well)
- Breaking out with trend (continuation) is higher probability

## Adam & Eve (Double Bottom Variant)

### Description
Double bottom where first bottom is V-shaped (Adam) and second is rounded (Eve). The contrast shows selling exhaustion.

### Identification Criteria
- [ ] Prior downtrend
- [ ] First bottom (Adam): Sharp V-shape, spike low
- [ ] Rally between bottoms to form neckline
- [ ] Second bottom (Eve): Rounded, wider base
- [ ] Eve bottom at similar or slightly higher level than Adam
- [ ] Volume: High on Adam spike, decreasing through Eve

### Entry Rules
- **Trigger**: Break above neckline (peak between the two bottoms)
- **Volume**: Increased volume on breakout confirms

### Stop Placement
- Below Eve's low
- Or below neckline after retest

### Target Calculation
- **Measured Move**: Distance from bottom to neckline, projected up
- **T1**: 50% measured move
- **T2**: 100% measured move

### Ideal Setup
- Clear contrast between sharp Adam and rounded Eve
- Eve shows accumulation (rounding indicates buying)
- Higher low on Eve vs Adam is bullish

## Ascending Triangle

### Description
Continuation pattern with horizontal resistance and rising support (higher lows). Shows buyers increasingly aggressive.

### Identification Criteria
- [ ] Horizontal resistance line with 2+ touches
- [ ] Rising support line (higher lows) with 2+ touches
- [ ] Price compressing into apex
- [ ] Volume decreasing as pattern develops
- [ ] Typically breaks out 50-75% through pattern

### Entry Rules
- **Trigger**: Close above horizontal resistance
- **Volume**: Should expand on breakout
- **Failed breakout**: If price re-enters pattern, exit

### Stop Placement
- Below most recent higher low
- Or below rising trendline

### Target Calculation
- **Measured Move**: Height of triangle at base, projected from breakout
- Conservative: Use height at breakout point

### Ideal Setup
- In context of existing uptrend (continuation)
- At least 4 weeks of development
- Multiple tests of resistance (shows it's significant)
- Clear higher lows (not messy)

## Bull Flag

### Description
Continuation pattern after strong upward move. Tight consolidation (flag) that slopes slightly down before resuming uptrend.

### Identification Criteria
- [ ] Prior strong move up (flagpole): 10-30%+ move
- [ ] Tight parallel channel consolidation
- [ ] Flag slopes down or sideways (NOT up)
- [ ] Duration: 1-4 weeks typically (short relative to pole)
- [ ] Volume: Decreases during flag, expands on breakout

### Entry Rules
- **Trigger**: Break above upper flag boundary
- **Volume**: Must increase on breakout
- **Timing**: Often breaks out on 2nd or 3rd test of upper boundary

### Stop Placement
- Below flag low
- Or below lower flag boundary

### Target Calculation
- **Measured Move**: Length of flagpole projected from breakout point
- Often the second leg equals the first (flagpole)

### Ideal Setup
- Strong, clean flagpole (shows momentum)
- Tight flag (shallow pullback = strong hands)
- Flag duration less than flagpole duration
- Volume signature: High on pole, low in flag, high on break

## Pattern Comparison Quick Reference

| Pattern | Type | Duration | Best Volume Signal |
|---------|------|----------|-------------------|
| iH&S | Reversal | 6-20 weeks | Decreasing L→H→R, spike on break |
| Cup & Handle | Continuation | 7-65 weeks | Dry up in handle |
| Falling Wedge | Both | 3-12 weeks | Decreasing, spike on break |
| Adam & Eve | Reversal | 4-12 weeks | Spike on Adam, quiet Eve |
| Ascending Triangle | Continuation | 4-12 weeks | Decreasing, spike on break |
| Bull Flag | Continuation | 1-4 weeks | Low in flag, spike on break |
